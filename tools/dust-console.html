How DUST Generation Works

DUST is the gas token on Midnight. It is generated passively by holders of NIGHT on Cardano.

  • Each NIGHT generates DUST at 8,267 specks/star/sec
  • Max capacity is 5 DUST per NIGHT held
  • Full capacity reaches in ~7 days
  • DUST is non-transferable and decays when backing NIGHT is spent
  • Register your reward address at the Nethermind DUST Generator

Understanding this estimate

Generation: DUST is produced passively from staked NIGHT, capped at 5× an address's NIGHT balance. The figures above are a projection from the assumed staked-NIGHT amount — not a measured network total.

Why no consumption / net-flow / sustainability? Those need a real per-transaction DUST fee, which is not yet available from the indexer. Rather than show a guessed fee, those metrics are intentionally omitted. 1 DUST = 10^15 specks.
